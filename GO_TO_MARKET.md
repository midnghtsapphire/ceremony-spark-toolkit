# Go-To-Market

## Product category

AI-assisted workflow + commerce platform for wedding officiants and ceremony creators.

## Project value and goal alignment

Ceremony Spark Toolkit advances portfolio goals by pairing recurring subscriptions with digital product commerce in a focused niche. It reduces user churn risk by combining utility (script generation, legal guidance, checklists) and transaction workflows (checkout, account, admin) in one app.

## Research highlights (with sources)

1. **Stable ceremony demand base in the U.S.**
   - CDC marriage statistics show a persistent annual marriage volume, supporting durable demand for ceremony services.
   - Source: https://www.cdc.gov/nchs/fastats/marriage-divorce.htm

2. **Planning workflow complexity drives tooling demand**
   - Wedding planning workflows span legal, creative, and logistics tasks, creating opportunity for bundled tooling.
   - Source: https://www.theknot.com/content/wedding-statistics

3. **Subscription + checkout infrastructure lowers go-live friction**
   - Stripe + hosted checkout/customer portal patterns accelerate secure monetization.
   - Source: https://docs.stripe.com/billing/subscriptions/checkout

## ICP (ideal customer profile)

- Solo officiants
- Small officiant teams/agencies
- Ceremony coaches and script consultants

## Packaging and offers

- Free tier: limited script generation and resources
- Pro tier: full templates, legal guide depth, export capability
- Commerce add-ons: premium script packs and ceremony bundles

## Launch plan (single iteration)

1. **Week 1:** Deploy Vercel production + Supabase functions, run checkout smoke tests
2. **Week 2:** Publish launch content (demo video + officiant workflow examples)
3. **Week 3:** Outreach to officiant communities and wedding vendor groups
4. **Week 4:** Retention optimization (email onboarding + first-value milestones)

## 3-year commercial model (assumption-based)

- Target: 3,000 subscribers by year 3
- ARPU: $29/month
- Add-on conversion: 20% at $12/month equivalent
- Estimated year-3 run-rate: **$1.13M ARR**
