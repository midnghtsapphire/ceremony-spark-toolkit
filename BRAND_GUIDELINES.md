# Brand Guidelines

## Brand promise

Ceremony Spark Toolkit helps officiants deliver personalized ceremonies faster, with confidence and professional polish.

## Voice

- Clear
- Warm
- Practical
- Trustworthy

## Messaging pillars

1. **Create faster:** Generate and adapt ceremony scripts quickly.
2. **Stay compliant:** Access legal guidance and structured checklists.
3. **Monetize reliably:** Use integrated subscriptions and product sales.

## Visual direction

- Primary tone: professional with celebratory accents
- Prioritize readability and confidence over novelty
- Keep UI copy concise and action-oriented

## Positioning statement

For wedding officiants and ceremony professionals, Ceremony Spark Toolkit is a specialized script-and-commerce platform that combines planning, content creation, and recurring monetization in one workflow.
